# Football Decision Intelligence — Private Beta Runbook

## Purpose

The private beta is designed to validate whether Football Decision Intelligence provides useful tactical decision support for real football coaches, analysts and performance staff.

The system does not replace the coach. It structures tactical alternatives, surfaces assumptions and risks, provides verified AI explanations, and records the coach's final decision and subsequent assessment.

## Production Coach Application

Production Coach UI:

https://coach-ui-production.up.railway.app

Access is restricted to invited private-beta participants.

## Target Pilot Users

Initial pilot participants should preferably include:

- Football coaches
- Assistant coaches
- Performance analysts
- Tactical analysts
- Academy coaching staff
- Other practitioners involved in match preparation or in-game tactical decision-making

## Pilot Workflow

Each participant should:

1. Open the Coach Dashboard.
2. Enter the private-beta access code.
3. Create a team profile.
4. Enter a realistic tactical match scenario.
5. Review the deterministic Tactical Decision Brief.
6. Optionally run the Verified AI Explanation.
7. Record the tactical option they would choose.
8. Record or simulate the subsequent match outcome.
9. Review Team History.
10. Complete the Pilot Feedback form.

## Suggested Test Scenario

Participants should ideally use a real or realistic tactical situation from their own coaching experience rather than simply copying the demonstration scenario.

Examples include:

- Protecting a one-goal lead
- Chasing a game late
- Dealing with repeated wide overloads
- Protecting a booked defender
- Responding to an opponent formation change
- Maintaining midfield control
- Managing a red-card scenario
- Balancing defensive security against attacking threat

## Pilot Questions

After using the product, establish:

1. Was the tactical decision brief understandable?
2. Did the alternatives reflect realistic coaching options?
3. Did the system surface anything the coach had not immediately considered?
4. Was the verified AI explanation useful?
5. Were any recommendations unrealistic or misleading?
6. What information would the coach want the system to know that it currently does not?
7. Would the coach use this for match preparation, live decision review, or post-match analysis?
8. What would need to change before regular use?
9. Would the coach join a longer private pilot?
10. Would the coach or organisation potentially pay for the product?

## Commercial Validation

The current willingness-to-pay question is exploratory only.

No payment is collected through the application.

Evidence to capture includes:

- Number of invited coaches
- Number who opened the application
- Number who completed a scenario
- Number who completed pilot feedback
- Number interested in continued pilot participation
- Indicative willingness to pay
- Most requested features
- Repeated usability problems
- Evidence of repeat use

## Success Criteria for Initial Private Beta

The first validation milestone is not large-scale adoption.

A useful initial result would be:

- 3–5 credible football practitioners testing the product
- At least 3 completed tactical scenarios
- At least 3 structured feedback responses
- At least 1 practitioner willing to test again
- At least 1 positive willingness-to-pay signal or a clear explanation of what would be required before payment
- No critical reliability or safety failure during testing

## Operational Monitoring

The private Operations Dashboard should be used to monitor:

- Verified AI runs
- Approval rate
- Verification failures
- Provider failures
- Latency
- Token usage
- Model usage

The Operations Dashboard must not be shared with pilot participants.

## Safety and Product Positioning

Football Decision Intelligence is a decision-support system.

The coach remains responsible for the final tactical decision.

AI-generated explanations are exposed only after the configured verification process passes.

Historical coach assessments are descriptive and do not prove that a tactical intervention caused a match result.
